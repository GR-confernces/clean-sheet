Note

  • Drop an Excel file, or paste a Google Sheet link. Either one is enough.
  • The email column is found automatically, from a header such as Email or Mail Id, or from the column with the most @ addresses.
  • Duplicates are matched by email. A different name does not keep a second row with the same email.
  • The first row for each email stays. Later copies are removed in full.
  • Rows with a blank email stay. Capital and lowercase letters count as the same email.
  • Excel files are cleaned in your browser. A Google Sheet opens only when sharing is set to Anyone with the link.
  • The sheet with the most emails is cleaned. Other sheets stay in the download as they are.
